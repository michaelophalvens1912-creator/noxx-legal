Request account deletion

To request deletion of your NOXX account and its associated personal data, email mcorecreative@gmail.com with the subject NOXX Account Deletion Request. State the email address associated with the NOXX account. Do not send your password or payment-card details.

Email account deletion request

What happens next?

  1. We acknowledge your request and may ask for reasonable verification that you control the account.
  2. After verification, we process deletion of the account and associated gameplay/profile information, including stored avatar ownership and virtual-credit state, where technically held and legally erasable.
  3. Some minimal information may be retained if required for legal compliance, fraud prevention, disputes or security; we explain any such retention if it applies.
  4. We confirm completion or explain any lawful reason for additional processing.

Subscriptions and purchases

Deleting an NOXX account does not automatically cancel any subscription managed by Google Play. If you have one, cancel it separately through Google Play subscription management. Virtual credits have no cash-out value; deletion may permanently remove unused entitlements, subject to applicable consumer rights.

Data deletion and support

Our Privacy Policy describes account and data processing. You may use this web page even after uninstalling the app. If you cannot access your sign-in email, write to mcorecreative@gmail.com explaining the issue without providing passwords.

Important before Play publication: Configure an actual administrative workflow that can verify a request and delete the account and associated data across the authentication provider, app database and any service providers. An email link is only the request method, not automatic deletion.
