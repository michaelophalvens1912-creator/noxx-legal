Request account deletion

To request deletion of your NOXX account and its associated personal data, email mcorecreative@gmail.com with the subject NOXX Account Deletion Request. State the email address associated with the NOXX account. Do not send your password or payment-card details.

Email account deletion request

What happens next?

  1. We acknowledge your request and may ask for reasonable verification that you control the account.
  2. After verification, we arrange deletion of your Firebase Authentication record and information under our control that can be identified and lawfully erased. The current app stores avatars, credits, rewards and game progress locally on the device, rather than in an active production database. We will provide instructions for clearing local data on any devices where it remains.
  3. Some minimal information may be retained if required for legal compliance, fraud prevention, disputes or security; we explain any such retention if it applies.
  4. We confirm when deletion steps under our control are complete, or explain any lawful reason for additional handling. Deletion is not yet automatic in the app.

Subscriptions and purchases

NOXX currently has no active Google Play Billing subscriptions. If subscriptions are introduced, deleting an account will not automatically cancel a Google Play subscription; it must be cancelled separately. Virtual credits have no cash-out value. Clearing local app data may permanently remove unspent credits and game progress, subject to applicable consumer rights.

Data deletion and support

Our Privacy Policy describes account and data processing. You may use this web page even after uninstalling the app. If you cannot access your sign-in email, write to mcorecreative@gmail.com explaining the issue without providing passwords.

Current process: This page lets you request deletion by email. NOXX does not yet have an automatic in-app account deletion function. Never send passwords or payment details. Reasonable verification may be required before removing a Firebase account.
