2. What information may be processed

Google sign-in uses Firebase Authentication (project noxx-9b468). Account-related information may include your Google UID, display name, email address, profile-photo URL and sign-in/account timestamps. NOXX stores profile copies, avatar ownership and selection, virtual-credit balances, reward claims, game progress, preferences and certain gameplay or transaction records in browser/device localStorage, separated by user ID. Optional locally stored records can include support drafts and browser/window settings. Support emails are processed when you contact us. There is currently no active Google Play Billing checkout in the app, and we do not collect payment-card numbers through NOXX.

Current storage: Sign-in uses Firebase Authentication; gameplay and profile information is currently saved in localStorage on your device. This local information can differ between devices and may be lost if app or browser storage is cleared. A production server-side wallet is not yet configured.

3. Why we use information and legal bases

We process sign-in and gameplay data to provide the service (performance of a contract), respond to support and privacy requests (contract or legitimate interests as applicable), and help protect accounts and prevent misuse (legitimate interests or legal obligations as applicable). The reviewed app code did not show active advertising tracking, Crashlytics or active Analytics measurement. If these features are introduced, this notice will be updated and any legally required consent obtained.

4. Service providers and transfers

Google and Firebase provide sign-in and authentication services. The reviewed app code did not show active Firestore, Firebase Storage, Cloud Functions or push notifications. Gameplay and profile information is stored on your device; Firebase authentication records are processed through Google infrastructure. Google may process data internationally subject to applicable terms and safeguards. We do not sell personal information to advertisers. This notice will be updated if production services change.

5. How long information is kept

Local profile and gameplay data currently remain on your device until app or browser storage is cleared or an available local removal process is used. Firebase Authentication account records remain until the account is deleted from Firebase Authentication. An automatic deletion function and fixed deletion timetable are not yet implemented. We handle deletion requests by email, verify account ownership as needed, and explain which records can be deleted or must lawfully be retained. Some support or legal correspondence may require retention under applicable obligations.

6. Security

We aim to use authentication, access controls and reasonable safeguards to restrict access and prevent accidental loss or unauthorized access. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Report security concerns to mcorecreative@gmail.com.

7. Your rights

Subject to the GDPR and applicable law, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability where applicable, or object to certain processing. You may also complain to the Croatian data protection authority (AZOP) or another competent supervisory authority. Contact us to exercise your rights; identity verification may be necessary to protect the account.

8. Account deletion

To request deletion, visit Delete Account and email us. NOXX does not currently delete Firebase Authentication accounts automatically. Uninstalling NOXX does not itself remove the Firebase account; local data on other devices may need to be cleared there separately. We explain any legally necessary retention when processing your request.
