HOW TO HOST (GitHub Pages)
1. Create a PUBLIC repository, for example noxx-legal (no account secrets).
2. Upload ONLY these five website files to its repository root.
3. Settings > Pages > Deploy from branch > main / (root) > Save.
4. Once published, check the public Terms, Privacy and Delete Account URLs.
5. Paste the exact public URLs in Google Play Console, only after checking the legal drafts.

CHECK BEFORE PUBLISHING
[ ] Correct legal status: individual or registered business; name/address and legally required registration identifiers; any mandatory phone contact.
[ ] Accurate published pricing, purchase and subscription descriptions.
[ ] The app really has no cash-out or real-world-money winnings.
[ ] Verify all Firebase, Google login, analytics, billing, hosting, third parties, retention and transfer practices.
[ ] Confirm account deletion process works in practice (not just email); legal retention and backup policy.
[ ] Confirm appropriate age rating, game/gambling policy and odds disclosures for purchased random rewards.
[ ] Fill Google Play Data safety truthfully based on implementation.
[ ] Consider Croatian/EU professional legal review before launch.
